AI Video Pipeline

A personal production tool for AI-generated short videos.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service

What it is

AI Video Pipeline is a private, self-hosted tool developed and operated by a single individual. It helps the operator write short stories and songs, generate illustrations, voices and video clips with AI models on the operator's own computer, assemble them into short videos, and publish those videos to the operator's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

The tool is not offered as a service, has no other users and does not access anyone else's account. All published videos are labelled as AI-generated (altered or synthetic content).

How it uses YouTube and TikTok

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploading the operator's videos to the operator's channel, updating their titles and descriptions, reading view and engagement statistics of these videos, and reading and replying to comments on them.
  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): uploading the operator's videos to the operator's TikTok account as drafts or posts.

See the Privacy Policy for exactly what data is accessed, how it is stored and how access can be revoked.
